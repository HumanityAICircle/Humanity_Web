Common questions, clear answers

Where should I begin?

Choose one repetitive low-risk process, such as drafting a generic product description. Set a measurable quality and time baseline.

What should I never fully automate?

Avoid unsupervised high-impact decisions about employment, credit, legal rights, safety, payments or sensitive customer matters.

How do I evaluate return on investment?

Measure total time saved minus checking and correction time, tooling cost, and error cost. A flashy demo is not an ROI measurement.

How do I protect customer trust?

Explain appropriate AI use, review customer-facing outputs, and limit access to customer information.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
