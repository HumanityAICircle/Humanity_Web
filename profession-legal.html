How can AI help legal work without inventing authority?

Use AI to assist research organization and draft structure, never to replace source verification or lawyer accountability.

Common questions, clear answers

Can I trust AI case citations?

No. Verify every case, quotation and proposition from an authoritative legal database or original judgment.

Can I upload client files?

Only with appropriate authorization and an approved secure service consistent with professional confidentiality duties.

Where could I start?

Try a hypothetical chronology or issue checklist with fabricated facts, then compare against verified legal sources.

Will AI replace legal judgment?

AI may accelerate portions of drafting and review, but decisions about advice, strategy and professional responsibilities require qualified humans.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
