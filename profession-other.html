Common questions, clear answers

I am completely new. What is AI?

AI is a family of systems that identify patterns or generate outputs from data. Current generative tools can produce fluent text and images but still make mistakes.

Should I be scared or excited?

Both feelings are understandable. Focus on what matters in your circumstances, verify claims and learn with others at your own pace.

How can I try it safely?

Use a publicly available, low-stakes example such as a fictional travel packing checklist. Check results before relying on them.

Do I have to join the community?

No. All public learning pages and FAQs are available without registration.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
