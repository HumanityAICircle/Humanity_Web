Common questions, clear answers

Can I rely on AI medical answers?

Generic AI responses can be incorrect or outdated. Clinical decisions need approved tools, appropriate evidence and professional review.

Can I share patient records with AI?

Not with an unapproved system. Follow applicable privacy rules, institutional policies and explicit authorizations.

What is a safer starting point?

Try non-clinical, synthetic scenarios for training or plain-language drafting and verify every claim.

How do I assess a medical AI product?

Look for regulatory status where applicable, clinical validation, performance on relevant populations, privacy safeguards and error escalation.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
