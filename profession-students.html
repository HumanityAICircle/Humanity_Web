Common questions, clear answers

Do I still need to learn fundamentals?

Yes. AI can generate plausible answers that are wrong. Understanding the subject lets you spot mistakes and explain the reasoning.

What should I learn first?

Start with one field you care about, learn how AI tools work at a basic level, practise checking sources, and complete a small project.

Will there still be entry-level jobs?

Opportunities will differ by occupation and place. Develop a portfolio demonstrating real problem-solving and human review rather than relying on AI-generated work alone.

How do I use AI without cheating?

Follow your school or university rules. Use AI for explanations, practice questions and feedback where allowed, and disclose assistance when required.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
