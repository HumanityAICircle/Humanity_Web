# Humanity AI Circle V18 — Fresh Lens Homepage

## Design decisions
- Brand and color palette retained, with a new editorial layout and tightened vertical spacing.
- V7 human-first headline remains the primary message.
- Homepage simplified to six sections and four interactive questions.
- Technical evolution timeline and oversized survey bars removed from homepage; supporting pages retained.
- Existing community illustration reused; no invented member photos or testimonials.
- All supporting pages, legacy assets, links and global navigation JavaScript retained.
- Non-promotional pathways to WhatsApp, Instagram, gatherings and stories.
