How will AI change my work?

AI is more likely to change tasks unevenly than every job at once. Begin with your actual weekly tasks, not predictions about your job title.

Common questions, clear answers

Will AI replace my job?

No one can determine that from a job title alone. List your tasks: repetitive drafting and sorting may be easier to automate; relationship building, judgment, accountability and handling exceptions still need human oversight. Industry, employer and regulation matter.

Which skills should I develop?

Learn to frame problems, check AI output, protect sensitive information, and improve one work process. Then add role-specific domain knowledge and practical tool literacy.

Can I put office data into AI?

Only into tools approved by your organization and only in ways permitted by your data rules. Never paste private employee, client, financial or customer information into an unapproved service.

How can I start this week?

Pick one low-risk task, write the expected result, try an approved tool using non-sensitive inputs, verify the output, and record whether it saved time without lowering quality.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
