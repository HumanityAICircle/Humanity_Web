Can AI support HR without making unfair decisions?

Use AI cautiously for routine support; keep privacy, fairness, policy accuracy and human review central.

Common questions, clear answers

Can I use AI to screen candidates?

Hiring tools may trigger employment and privacy obligations. Require legal review, bias testing, accessibility and accountable oversight before any consequential deployment.

What are low-risk HR uses?

Drafting anonymized FAQ explanations and generic onboarding checklists can be safer starting points when verified against current policy.

Can I upload employee records?

Do not use unapproved AI services for personal employment information. Follow employer security and data governance rules.

How do I explain an AI-assisted answer?

Show the applicable policy source, date and human escalation path. Avoid inventing company policy.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
