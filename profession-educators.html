How can I teach responsibly with AI?

Use AI to support teachers and learners without outsourcing learning, fairness or professional judgment.

Common questions, clear answers

Will AI replace teachers?

Teaching involves relationships, assessment, safeguarding and adapting to learners. AI may help prepare materials, but educators remain accountable for their classroom decisions.

What can I try first?

Use a fictional example to draft differentiated practice questions. Review all facts, age suitability, accessibility and curriculum alignment before using them.

How should I address student AI use?

Explain what use is allowed, how to acknowledge assistance, and what students must demonstrate independently.

Is it safe to upload student information?

Avoid uploading identifying student information unless your institution has explicitly approved the system and data-handling practices.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
