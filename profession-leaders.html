Common questions, clear answers

Should I mandate AI use?

First check the task, tool suitability, training and data policies. Allow a safe route to flag errors and concerns.

What should our AI policy cover?

Approved tools, prohibited data, human sign-off, quality checks, incident reporting, accessibility and ownership of decisions.

How do I measure outcomes?

Track quality, time, user impact, error rate and employee experience together, not just adoption.

How should I discuss jobs and anxiety?

Be specific about known changes and uncertainty. Invite employees to identify useful tasks and retain clear human accountability.

Try a safe 15-minute exercise

Write down one routine task or question related to your role. Describe what a useful result would look like. Use a fictional or publicly shareable example in an approved AI tool, check the output against a reliable source, and record one benefit and one limitation. Do not upload confidential data.
